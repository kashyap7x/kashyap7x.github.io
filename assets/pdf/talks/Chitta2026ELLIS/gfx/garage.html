kesai-labs / py123d_garage

Data. Training. Benchmarking. A starter kit for end-to-end driving research.

Setup | Data | Training | Evaluation

Py123D Garage is an open-source framework to train end-to-end driving policies and evaluate them in closed loop. Built on 123D, it has three parts: one interface to large real-world driving datasets, a fast training loop, and closed-loop simulators that recreate the challenges of real AV deployment. It is the reference starting point for the Alpasim E2E Challenge 2026, with pretrained baselines to build on.
